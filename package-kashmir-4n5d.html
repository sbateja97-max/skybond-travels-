📅 Tour Plans

Day 1 – Srinagar → Pahalgam

Airport pickup, visit saffron fields, apple orchards and bat factory, then continue to Pahalgam. Optional Aru and Betaab Valley visits.

Day 2 – Pahalgam → Gulmarg

Scenic transfer via Tangmarg and hotel check-in.

Day 3 – Gulmarg Sightseeing

Enjoy Gulmarg and optional Gondola, golf course, Rani Temple, church or pony ride.

Day 4 – Gulmarg → Sonmarg

Scenic drive via Gagangir and optional Thajwas Glacier visit by local taxi.

Day 5 – Sonmarg → Srinagar

Breakfast, checkout and airport transfer.
