📅 Tour Plans

Day 1 – Paro Arrival → Thimphu

Meet your representative at Paro International Airport and enjoy the scenic transfer to Thimphu.

Day 2 – Thimphu Sightseeing

Visit Buddha Point, Takin Preserve, Postal Museum, Simply Bhutan, School of Arts & Crafts, paper factory, nunnery and textile museum.

Day 3 – Punakha Excursion

Drive through Dochula Pass and visit the magnificent Punakha Dzong before returning to Thimphu.

Day 4 – Thimphu → Paro

Transfer to Paro and visit Ta Dzong / National Museum.

Day 5 – Tiger’s Nest or Chele La

Take the famous Tiger’s Nest hike or choose Chele La Pass, subject to the confirmed itinerary.

Day 6 – Departure

Transfer to Paro International Airport.
